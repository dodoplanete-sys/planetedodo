Our approach

Sleep Education™: A Discipline,
Not a One-Size-Fits-All Method

Sleep Education™ and integrative sleep therapy™ — the disciplines at the heart of our practice — go far beyond standard sleep advice. They account for every dimension of the child and family to build a lasting, tailor-made, deeply human solution. No two children are alike. No two solutions should be either.

"Sleep isn't a problem to fix. It's a balance to restore — together, step by step, with kindness."

The 8 dimensions of our support

Sleep neuroscience

Understanding circadian rhythms, sleep cycles, and a child's neurological maturation to act at the right moment, in the right way.

Attachment & parental bond

Sleep is built in emotional safety. We work on attachment as a core pillar of healthy, peaceful sleep.

Nutrition & microbiome

Diet, digestion, and gut health directly influence sleep quality — a frequently overlooked dimension that's central to our approach.

Emotional regulation

Night fears, separation anxiety, evening hyperactivity — we help children regulate their emotions to access calm, restful sleep.

Sleep environment

Light, temperature, noise, space — the physical environment deeply shapes sleep quality. We optimize every parameter.

Biological rhythms

Chronotypes, sleep windows, nap schedules — aligning routines with a child's natural rhythms changes everything.

Family dynamics

Parents are at the heart of the process. We equip, guide, and empower them — lasting change is born within the family.

Integrative medicine

Our approach integrates developmental psychology, naturopathy, and mind-body therapies for a truly holistic, whole-child support.

Our practice is rooted in the Sleep Education™ and Integrative Sleep Therapy™ of Institut SOMNA — a certifying program in natural sleep management, recognized by 13 professional associations and present in 13 countries.

Services

Every age has its challenges —
every family needs its own solution

Sleep evolves deeply at each stage of development. Our programs are designed specifically for each age group, accounting for the unique biological, emotional, and family realities of each period.

0 – 4 months

Newborn

Understand, not correct

At this stage, sleep is still neurologically maturing. The goal is not to 'make' the baby sleep — it's to help parents decode their baby's signals and gently lay secure, loving foundations.

  • Reading hunger and tiredness cues
  • Establishing gentle, consistent routines
  • Safe co-sleeping support (7 criteria)
  • Managing day/night confusion
  • Reassurance and support for exhausted parents
4 – 18 months

Baby

Consolidate, not force

Sleep regressions, falling-asleep associations, collapsing nap schedules, hourly wake-ups — this period is full of challenges. We build strategies that genuinely respect your baby's unique biological rhythm.

  • Managing sleep regressions (4, 8, 12, 18 months)
  • Building sleep independence without cry-it-out
  • Bassinet → crib/bed transition
  • Night and nap rhythm balance by age
  • Alternative sleep associations
18 months – 5 years

Toddler

Security that sets them free

Night fears, bedtime resistance, separation anxiety, monsters under the bed — this is the age of big imagination and big emotions. We create meaningful rituals and tools tuned to their inner world.

  • Meaningful bedtime rituals for safety and calm
  • Managing fears and nighttime anxiety
  • Gentle nap weaning at the right time
  • Guided stories, visualizations and transitional objects
  • Building bedroom independence
5 – 12 years

School-age child

Tools for a lifetime

Screens, school stress, night waking, difficulty winding down — at this age, children can become active agents of their own sleep. We give them tools they'll carry their whole lives.

  • Sleep hygiene and screen time management
  • School anxiety and performance stress
  • Behavioral insomnia and night waking
  • Meditation, breathwork and sensory tools
  • Personalized sleep plan for the child

About

A mom, a passion,
a mission

Luisa Suarez
Sleep Educator · Integrative Therapist
Institut SOMNA 13 associations Ages 0–12
View SOMNA Network Code of Ethics →

"My journey with sleep began the day I left my career behind to be fully present for every moment of my daughter's growth — and it was there, surrounded by extraordinary families, that this fascination was born."

Before becoming a mom, I worked in technology. When my daughter was born, my whole world shifted — and so did my priorities. My career was no longer what mattered most; what mattered was being truly present for her, in every moment of her growth. I was lucky enough to open a subsidized home daycare in my own house, so I could live that fully.

It was there, through the stories of the extraordinary families I got to know — and through my own experience — that sleep became a genuine fascination for me. Children's sleep, adults' sleep, the way it changes an entire family's life. It's the foundation of everything: even before eating or drinking, sleep is what changes everything. That fascination led me to train in Sleep Education.

My training and ethics

That desire led me to certify as a sleep educator and integrative therapist at Institut SOMNA — a certifying program in Sleep Education™ and integrative sleep therapy™, recognized by 13 professional associations. A 275-hour program rooted in neuroscience, developmental psychology, and integrative medicine.

My mission

Helping as many families as possible reclaim their sleep — and through it, reconnect with their children and with their own lives. A family that sleeps well laughs more, connects deeper, and thrives. I'm not here to judge you, but to walk beside you — step by step — toward gentler nights and lighter days.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What exactly is Sleep Education™?
Sleep Education™ is a dedicated discipline focused on children's sleep — not a method to apply mechanically. Developed by Institut SOMNA, it integrates sleep neuroscience, developmental psychology, nutrition, attachment theory, and integrative medicine to build a lasting, gentle, medication-free solution with each family. It's an approach recognized by 13 professional associations.
Is your approach compatible with breastfeeding and co-sleeping?
Absolutely. We fully respect your parenting choices — breastfeeding, co-sleeping, or any other practice. Our support adapts to your reality and your values. We don't ask you to change what works for your family; we build strategies around who you are.
Are consultations available worldwide?
Yes! All consultations take place online, by video call. Whether you're in North America, Europe, Latin America, or anywhere else in the world, Planète Dodo can support you. Sessions are available in French, English, and Spanish.
At what age can we start?
From birth! We support families from the newborn period through 12 years old. At every stage, the sleep challenges differ — biologically, emotionally, developmentally — and our approach adapts entirely.
Do you use cry-it-out methods?
Never. Our approach is entirely based on gentleness, respect for the child's temperament, and collaboration with parents. We never use any method that involves leaving a child to cry without response. The goal is always to find the most respectful solution — for your child and for you.
Do you offer a free consultation?
Yes! I offer a free 20-minute introductory call, by phone or video. It's a chance to connect, talk about your situation, and see together whether my support is the right fit — with no commitment required.
